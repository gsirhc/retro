Site Information

This site emulates computer hardware running entirely in your browser. The emulator cores are written from scratch in C++ and compiled to a WebAssembly module. The module is downloaded to the browser, allowing the emulator to execute all hardware simulation locally. There are no virtual machines or third-party emulator engines involved.

Why not release more games/emulators now?

Development is quite a bit ahead of the current releases. However, the games/emulators are being released slowly to help vet them for stability and performance. Stay tuned for more releases. If you are super excited for more, let me know below!

Software Statement

This site does not host commercial software. All software provided is shareware, open source, or freely licensed stand-ins. You can (once released) provide your own commercial software in the emulators just like you would on a real machine.
