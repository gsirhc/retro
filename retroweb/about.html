Help / About

Vintage machines, running entirely in your browser.

This site contains era-correct microcomputer and arcade emulators simulating hardware from the 1970s to the 1990s. The goal here is to emulate the physical hardware as accurately and realistically as possible.

How the site works

The hardware emulation is written from scratch in C++ and compiled to a WebAssembly module so the system runs natively in your browser. There are no virtual machines or 3rd party emulator engines involved. Where applicable, hard drive and ROM images are automatically saved in the browser's IndexedDB storage so you don't lose your data. Note that none of your ROMs, floppy disks, hard disks or cassette/paper tapes are uploaded to our servers. Everything is stored only in your browser or your Google Drive account.

High-Scores: For arcade games with high-scores, the emulators will store your scores in browser storage so they persist across sessions. You can reset your high-scores at any time.

Google Drive

Some emulators provide integration with Google Drive which is currently by invitation only. Please contact me below if you'd like to use this feature.

Why are software and game ROMs not included?

The bottom line is that this site does not host proprietary software. Instead, each emulator implements era-correct virtual storage devices for loading your personal copies of the software. To load software, obtain a simple binary dump of the physical media then follow the instructions on the device. Each machine loads the media as it would in real life.

I do realize that not including the fun software increases the barrier-of-entry for the site but I'm not going for the casual scroller. I'm looking to engage the enthusiast/hobbyist who will take the time to get the software. That said, if you have questions, contact me below.

Browser Support

Chrome or Edge are recommended as most testing is done in Chrome however, the site should run fine in all major browsers. You may notice issues with sound, mouse, fullscreen or browser storage if you use privacy extensions or privacy focused browsers. If you do encounter issues first check your browser extensions, then contact me below if you believe its a general browser issue.

Privacy Statement

This site does not collect your identity, data or usage. The Google Drive feature is completely opt-in and only connects when you choose to use it. Your ROMs and media files stay entirely on your device or directly in Google. Nothing is uploaded to this site's servers.

Use of AI

The work for this site is done in conjunction with Artificial Intelligence (AI), in particular, Claude and Cursor. I am an old-school professional programmer but I found that AI could allow me to build complex hardware emulation without poring over dense specifications. So, I decided to take on the role of a software architect where I prompt the AI agents to build the software how I envision it.

I know there are AI haters out there but I invite you to check out the site anyway. I've done my best to assure there is no AI-slop. So, any bugs you find should be traditional in nature. If AI is really not your jam, thats fine. There are plenty of non-AI built emulators out there.
